Anillo Seals Data Portal

Terms of Use

Access to this information is restricted to academic and research purposes only. Users must acknowledge the source and the "Proyecto Anillo Seals ATE 220033" in any documentation, publications, or presentations resulting from the use of this data.

CTD AML

Oceanographic profiles including Conductivity, Temperature, and Depth (CTD) collected using AML sensors.

CTD NKE Time Series

Long-term time series of oceanographic parameters measured with NKE instrumentation platforms.

CTD SDLR

High-resolution vertical profiles and specialized CTD data from SDLR sensor systems.

DO Time Series

Continuous Dissolved Oxygen concentration monitoring across various depths and locations.

Discharge Time Series

Freshwater inflow, river discharge measurements, and estimated continental runoff datasets.

Meteo Time Series

Local meteorological conditions including wind speed/direction, air temperature, and atmospheric pressure.

Sealevel Time Series

Sea level measurements, tidal oscillations, and long-period wave analysis datasets.
